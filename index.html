    MANUFACTURING TIMELINE

    FROM IDEA TO FINISHED PRODUCT.

    A structured 8-stage manufacturing workflow ensuring repeatable precision, strict quality verification, and reliable delivery for global intimate apparel brands.

    01

    DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT

    Tech-pack evaluation, 3D pattern drafting, grading across size scales, and material architecture planning.

    02

    FABRIC & MATERIAL SELECTION

    Sourcing skin-safe microfibers, OEKO-TEX certified cottons, stretch laces, elastics, and hypoallergenic hardware.

    03

    SAMPLING & FIT APPROVAL

    Pre-production proto creation, fit mannequin analysis, stretch recovery checks, and brand sign-off.

    04

    PRECISION CUTTING

    Laser and automated knife cutting calibrated for delicate fabrics, ensuring zero fraying and symmetric lace motif alignment.

    05

    STITCHING & ASSEMBLY

    Specialized flatlock, zigzag, underwire channel casing, and stitchless ultrasonic bonding by skilled operators.

    06

    QUALITY CONTROL

    100% in-line visual inspection, seam tensile tests, dimensional measurement checks, and needle detection scanning.

    07

    FINISHING & STEAMING

    Form steaming, loose thread trimming, tagless heat-seal label application, and soft garment pressing.

    08

    PACKAGING & DISPATCH

    Custom branded hangtagging, individual polybagging/eco-boxes, barcode carton labeling, and dispatch coordination.
